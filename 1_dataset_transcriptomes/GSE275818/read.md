



GSE275818
Effects of defined voluntary running distances coupled with high-fat diet consumption on the murine skeletal muscle transcriptome (2025)



## Descriptions:
- C57BL/6 male
- Started at 3–4 weeks old; fed for 12 weeks
- Diet: normal-fat diet (NFD/AIN93G, lean control, sedentary) vs. high-fat diet (HFD, sedentary + exercise groups)
- Treatment: sedentary vs. voluntary wheel running (unrestricted ≈8.3 km/day or restricted to 75%/50%/25% of that distance)
- One time point (after 12 weeks HFD/NFD)
- 3–6 replicates per group (includes direct WT/lean NFD-sedentary vs. DIO/HFD-sedentary comparison)
